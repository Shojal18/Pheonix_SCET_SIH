# 🚢 PortIN
## Intelligent Freight Forecasting & Maritime Decision-Support Platform

PortIN is an intelligent maritime logistics and decision-support platform developed as a prototype for **Smart India Hackathon 2026**, addressing the challenge of optimizing vessel chartering and bulk cargo procurement for the East Coast of India.

The platform is designed to help **chartering, procurement, and logistics teams** make more informed decisions by bringing freight forecasting, vessel and route planning, cargo requirements, port conditions, weather considerations, and operational risk into one unified platform.

Instead of relying on a single freight-rate value or manually comparing multiple factors, PortIN provides a structured workflow to evaluate chartering strategies and identify potential cost, delay, and operational risks.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

### The Problem

Bulk cargo transportation involves several interconnected and uncertain factors:

- Fluctuating freight rates
- Changing fuel costs
- Cargo demand and market trends
- Vessel suitability and availability
- Port infrastructure limitations
- Weather-related disruptions
- Port congestion and waiting time
- Uncertainty in chartering decisions

A vessel that appears economical based only on freight price may not necessarily be suitable for a particular port, cargo requirement, or operating condition.

### 💡 Our Solution

PortIN combines these factors into a single decision-support platform.

Users can provide key voyage requirements such as:

- Cargo type
- Cargo quantity
- Origin port
- Destination port
- Laycan / shipping window
- Voyage requirements
- Vessel preferences

The system processes these inputs and provides decision-oriented insights including:

- Freight forecasting
- Vessel and route planning
- Port-vessel compatibility
- Chartering strategy comparison
- Estimated voyage cost
- Operational risk assessment
- Weather-related risk consideration
- Congestion analysis
- Idle-time analysis
- Simulation confidence
- Detailed decision reports

# 🎯 Key Features

### 📈 Freight Forecasting

PortIN analyzes freight-related parameters to identify market trends and support better chartering decisions.

### 🚢 Vessel & Route Planner

The platform evaluates vessel and route requirements to support selection of suitable vessel options for a particular cargo movement.

### ⚓ Port Compatibility

Port and vessel parameters are considered together to reduce the possibility of selecting vessels that are unsuitable for the destination port.

### 💰 Chartering Strategy Comparison

Users can compare multiple chartering strategies based on cost, operational conditions, risk, and expected idle time.

### 🌦️ Weather Intelligence

Weather conditions can be considered as an operational risk factor when evaluating voyage scenarios and potential delays.

### 🚨 Risk Assessment

PortIN evaluates operational factors and generates a risk score to help users identify potential issues before making a chartering decision.

### ⏱️ Idle-Time Analysis

The platform estimates potential idle-time differences between chartering strategies to support more efficient vessel utilization.

### 📊 Interactive Dashboard

A centralized dashboard provides market insights, forecasts, vessel information, alerts, route analysis, and simulation results.

### 🔔 Alerts & Notifications

The system provides an alert and notification layer for important operational information.

### 📄 Detailed Simulation Reports

Users can review the assumptions, parameters, scenarios, and results behind a generated chartering comparison.
